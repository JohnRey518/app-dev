## Overview
*Before Sunrise* (1995) is a film directed by **Richard Linklater**, starring **Ethan Hawke** and **Julie Delpy**.  
It’s about two strangers — **Jesse**, an American, and **Céline**, a French woman who meet on a train and decide to spend one magical night walking around **Vienna**, just talking about life, love, and everything in between.

-------------------

### 💖 Why I Love It So Much   
I’m seriously obsessed with *Before Sunrise*. It’s not your typical romance — it’s **real**, **deep**, and feels like watching two souls connect in the most genuine way. This movie is not the typical romance movie you are expecting
because the whole movie is them just talking and having a conversation like they are getting to know each other. The conversations are so natural that it feels like eavesdropping on real people falling in love. And bro, their conversation
is really genuine like I swear you can feel the emotions between them. Also, if you love this movie you really need to watch the sequel of it, there's the Before Sunset and Before Midnight. I swear it's all masterpiece.
Some moments just *hit different*. Like when they sit by the river or that silent scene at the record store brooo pure cinema.

### 💭 Final Thoughts
Honestly bro, *Before Sunrise* just hits on a whole different level. It’s not just a love story — it’s like watching two strangers slowly fall for each other through words, not grand gestures. The movie makes you realize how rare it is to truly connect with someone, even for just one night. It’s slow, calm, and real — and that’s what makes it powerful.  

Every time I finish watching it, I just sit there in silence like, “Damn, I want something like that.” 😭 It’s the kind of movie that stays in your head, makes you overthink, and lowkey gives you hope that maybe, somewhere, there’s your own Céline or Jesse waiting to hop on the same train. 🚆💫

And bro, If I will be given a chance to watch a movie for the first time again, I will say *Before Sunrise* any day of the year no doubt. 😭😭😭
